# 60 powerful Claude (GenAI) prompts that can save you hours of work 

## Strategic Research & Decision-Making

PROMPT 1

I'm deciding between \[A\] and \[B\]. Real pros/cons - include

the stuff salespeople hide. Then tell me which you'd pick

and why.

  

PROMPT 2

I'm learning \[skill\]. Build me a Research \[topic\]. Give me 5

facts I probably don't know and 2 common myths most

people believe. Cite sources.

  

PROMPT 3

I'm about to buy \[ product\]. What 3 questions should I ask

before paying? What red flags should I watch for?

  

PROMPT 4

Help me decide: \[situation\]. Ask me 5 questions, one at a

time. After my answers, tell me what to do.

  

PROMPT 5

I'm considering \[big life move\]. List every risk I'm probably

not thinking about. Don't soften it.

  

PROMPT 6

Fact-check this: \[paste claim\]. What's true, what's

misleading, what's flat-out wrong. Link the sources.

  

  

## Work Productivity

  

PROMPT 1

Here are my tasks: \[ list\]. Rank by actual impact, not

urgency. Tell me the 2 to do today and which to drop

entirely.

  

PROMPT 2

Write the agenda for my \[ meeting type\] with \[person\]. 4

items max. End with one decision I need to make.

  

PROMPT 3

Summarize this meeting transcript: \[ paste\]. What was

decided, who owns what, what's still open.

  

PROMPT 4

I've been stuck on \[task\] for 2 hours. Ask me 5 questions to

figure out what's actually blocking me.

  

PROMPT 5

Write my weekly update for my boss. Here's what I did: \[list\].

3 bullets. Lead with outcomes, not activities.

  

PROMPT 6

I have 45 minutes before \[ event\]. What's the highest-

leverage thing I can do right now? Don't suggest

"organizing.'

  

  

## Emails That Feel Human

  

PROMPT 1

Write a follow-up email to \[person\] about \[topic\]. Under 60

words. No "I hope this finds you well" - start with something

specific they'd actually remember.

  

PROMPT 2

I got this email: \[paste\]. Draft 3 replies: short & friendly,

polite decline, and buying-time. Match the sender's tone.

  

PROMPT 3

Rewrite this email in my voice: \[paste\]. Make it sound like a

real person wrote it at 11pm, not a robot at 9am. Cut the fluff.

  

PROMPT 4

Draft a polite "no" email to \[ request\]. Grateful but firm. One-

sentence reason max. No over-explaining.

  

PROMPT 5

I need to apologize for \[situation\]. Write the email. Own it,

don't blame-shift, offer one concrete fix. Under 80 words.

  

PROMPT 6

Write a cold email to \[person\] pitching \[idea\]. Subject line +

3-sentence body. Start with why them, not why me.

## Difficult Conversations

  

PROMPT 1

My \[person\] said \[ thing\]. I'm upset but want to respond well.

Draft 2 replies - one calm, one honest.

  

PROMPT 2

I need to bring up \[issue\] with \[person\]. Write the exact

opening line. Direct but not aggressive.

  

PROMPT 3

I said something I regret: \[paste\]. Help me apologize

without making it about me. Under 100 words.

  

PROMPT 4

I need to end \[relationship/contract\]. Write the script. Kind,

clear, no room for negotiation.

  

PROMPT 5

I need to set a boundary with \[person\] about \[behavior\].

Write the message. Firm, not accusatory.

  

PROMPT 6

Someone's asking me for \[ favor I want to decline\]. Script a

"no" that doesn't burn the relationship.

  

## Creative Problem Solving

  

PROMPT 1

them: \[details\]. No gift cards, no flowers.

  

PROMPT 2

I want to \[goal\] but \[ obstacle\]. List 10 workarounds, from

obvious to ridiculous. Rank by feasibility.

  

PROMPT 3

<I'm stuck on \[problem\]. Give me 5 weird, unexpected angles

PROMPT 4

PROMPT 5

PROMPT 6

Play devil's advocate on \[my idea/plan\]. Where could this

fail? Assume I'm overconfident.

## Money and Finance

  

PROMPT 1

Here's my monthly spending: \[paste\]. Find the 3 biggest

leaks I'm not noticing. Be honest - don't be nice.

  

PROMPT 2

Explain \[financial term\] like I've never heard of it. Use a real

example with actual dollar amounts.

  

PROMPT 3

I earn $\[x\] and want to save for \[goal\]. Build me a realistic

6- month plan. No "skip your latte" advice.

  

PROMPT 4

Compare these two \[ investments/plans/cards\]. Which is

actually better for someone in \[ situation\]?

  

PROMPT 5

I'm negotiating \[salary/price\]. Give me the exact words to

use. Include what to say if they push back.

  

PROMPT 6

Help me understand this bill/contract: \[paste\]. What am I

paying for? What's hidden? What can I cut?
