title: 11 Laws of the Universe
tags: [reference]
source: local
date_added: 2026-07-16
description: Short list of systems laws that change how you plan work and write prompts.
---

1. Parkinson's Law — work expands to fill the time allowed; put a hard stop on the task.
2. Hofstadter's Law — it always takes longer than you expect, even when you take Hofstadter's Law into account.
3. Hanlon's Razor — do not attribute to malice what stupidity or incompetence explains.
4. Pareto Principle (80/20) — most of the effect comes from a few causes; find those first.
5. Peter Principle — people rise to their level of incompetence; design roles around work, not titles.
6. Hick's Law — more choices, slower decisions; cut the menu.
7. Goodhart's Law — when a measure becomes a target, it stops being a good measure.
8. Dunning–Kruger Effect — the least skilled are the most sure; demand evidence.
9. Occam's Razor — prefer the explanation with fewer assumptions.
10. Chesterton's Fence — do not remove a rule until you know why it was put there.
11. Brooks's Law — adding people to a late project makes it later.
