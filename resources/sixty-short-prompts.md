Collections like this stay in `resources/`. Split a line into `prompts/` only if it grows a real process.

## Strategic research and decisions
1. I'm deciding between [A] and [B]. Real pros/cons — include the stuff salespeople hide. Then tell me which you'd pick and why.
2. I'm learning [skill]. Build me research on [topic]. Give me 5 facts I probably don't know and 2 common myths. Cite sources.
3. I'm about to buy [product]. What 3 questions should I ask before paying? What red flags should I watch for?
4. Help me decide: [situation]. Ask me 5 questions, one at a time. After my answers, tell me what to do.
5. I'm considering [big life move]. List every risk I'm probably not thinking about. Don't soften it.
6. Fact-check this: [paste claim]. What's true, what's misleading, what's flat-out wrong. Link the sources.

## Work productivity
1. Here are my tasks: [list]. Rank by actual impact, not urgency. Tell me the 2 to do today and which to drop entirely.
2. Write the agenda for my [meeting type] with [person]. 4 items max. End with one decision I need to make.
3. Summarize this meeting transcript: [paste]. What was decided, who owns what, what's still open.
4. I've been stuck on [task] for 2 hours. Ask me 5 questions to figure out what's actually blocking me.
5. Write my weekly update for my boss. Here's what I did: [list]. 3 bullets. Lead with outcomes, not activities.
6. I have 45 minutes before [event]. What's the highest-leverage thing I can do right now? Don't suggest organizing.

## Emails that feel human
1. Write a follow-up email to [person] about [topic]. Under 60 words. No "I hope this finds you well" — start with something specific they'd remember.
2. I got this email: [paste]. Draft 3 replies: short and friendly, polite decline, and buying-time. Match the sender's tone.
3. Rewrite this email in my voice: [paste]. Make it sound like a person wrote it at 11pm. Cut the fluff.
4. Draft a polite "no" email to [request]. Grateful but firm. One-sentence reason max.
5. I need to apologize for [situation]. Own it, don't blame-shift, offer one concrete fix. Under 80 words.
6. Write a cold email to [person] pitching [idea]. Subject line + 3-sentence body. Start with why them, not why me.

## Learning
1. Explain [topic] like I'm smart but have zero background. Use everyday analogies. Define jargon inline.
2. I'm learning [skill]. Build a 7-day plan, 30 min/day. Day 1 is the most important thing. Day 7 I should be able to [outcome].
3. Quiz me on [topic]. Start easy, get harder. After each answer, tell me what I got wrong and what to study next.
4. I just read this: [paste or link]. Summarize in 5 bullets. Then tell me what the author probably left out.
5. Explain [topic] like I'm 12, then like a beginner adult, then like an expert. Compare the three.
6. Teach me [topic] using 3 real examples. No theory first.

## Difficult conversations
1. My [person] said [thing]. Draft 2 replies — one calm, one honest.
2. I need to bring up [issue] with [person]. Write the exact opening line. Direct but not aggressive.
3. I said something I regret: [paste]. Help me apologize without making it about me. Under 100 words.
4. I need to end [relationship/contract]. Write the script. Kind, clear, no room for negotiation.
5. I need to set a boundary with [person] about [behavior]. Firm, not accusatory.
6. Someone's asking me for [favor I want to decline]. Script a "no" that doesn't burn the relationship.

## Creative problem solving
1. Brainstorm gift ideas for [person]. Here's what I know: [details]. No gift cards, no flowers.
2. I want to [goal] but [obstacle]. List 10 workarounds, obvious to ridiculous. Rank by feasibility.
3. I'm stuck on [problem]. Give me 5 unexpected angles. No safe answers.
4. I need a name for [thing]. 20 options across playful, professional, weird, simple.
5. Pretend you're [expert type]. How would they approach [my problem]? What would they do first?
6. Play devil's advocate on [my idea/plan]. Where could this fail? Assume I'm overconfident.

## Life admin
1. I'm traveling to [place] for [days]. Build an itinerary a local would actually do.
2. Plan my week. Here's what I need to do: [list]. Block time realistically, including buffer.
3. I have [event] coming up. What do I prep, in what order, and when do I start each thing?
4. I need to [big annoying task]. Break it into 10 tiny steps. The first one takes 5 minutes.
5. Write my grocery list for [meal plan] this week. Organize by store section. Estimate total cost.

## Side hustle
1. I made $[x] last month from [side gig]. How do I double it without working 2x more hours?
2. Help me price my [service]. What should I charge, what's too low, and how do I justify it?
3. Based on these skills: [list], what are 5 side hustles I could start this weekend with $0?
4. I have $500 and want to test if [business idea] is real. Give me a 30-day plan to find out.
5. Write a one-page offer for selling [service] to [audience]. Hook, price, guarantee.
6. I have [X hours/week] free. Highest-paying use of that time for someone with [skill]?

## Writing and texting
1. Write a birthday message for [person]. Reference something specific about them.
2. Rewrite this text so I don't sound passive-aggressive: [paste]. Keep the meaning.
3. Draft 3 versions of this message: too cold, just right, too warm.
4. I wrote this: [paste]. Cut 40% of the words without losing anything important. Tell me what you cut and why.
5. Proofread this: [paste]. Flag anything unclear, awkward, or that sounds AI-generated.
6. Help me write a [wedding toast / eulogy / speech] for [person]. 2 minutes max. One story, one lesson, one thank-you.

## Money
1. Here's my monthly spending: [paste]. Find the 3 biggest leaks I'm not noticing.
2. Explain [financial term] like I've never heard of it. Use a real example with dollar amounts.
3. I earn $[x] and want to save for [goal]. Build a realistic 6-month plan. No "skip your latte."
4. Compare these two [investments/plans/cards]. Which is actually better for someone in [situation]?
5. I'm negotiating [salary/price]. Give me the exact words. Include the pushback line.
6. Help me understand this bill/contract: [paste]. What am I paying for? What's hidden? What can I cut?
